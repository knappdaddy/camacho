About us

A contractor that answers the phone and finishes the punch list.

American Restoration Tech was built around a simple frustration: most homeowners have to hire one company to clean up the damage and another to rebuild — and neither one takes responsibility for the seam between them.

Our story

Eighteen years, one standard.

We started as a two-person remodeling crew and added remediation capability because we kept opening walls and finding problems the homeowner had been told were "cosmetic." Rather than hand those jobs off, we got trained, certified and equipped to handle them ourselves.

Today we run renovation, restoration and site work out of one shop. A mold job can turn into a finished basement without a new contract or a new crew. A water loss gets dried, documented and rebuilt by people who were there on night one.

We are licensed, bonded and insured, EPA Lead-Safe certified, and trained to IICRC standards for water and mold. Every job is run by a named project lead you can reach directly, and we back our workmanship for two years.
