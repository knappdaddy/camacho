FAQ

Common questions.

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Walk-throughs and written estimates are free for residential projects in our service area.

How fast can you get here for a water emergency?

Our line is answered 24/7 and we are typically on site within two hours. Call rather than using the form if water is actively coming in.

Do you work with insurance companies?

Constantly. We document readings and photos from day one, write scopes in the format adjusters expect, and meet your adjuster on site. We work for you, not the carrier.
