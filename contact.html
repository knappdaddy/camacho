FAQ

Questions we get every week.

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Walk-throughs and written estimates are free for residential projects in our service area. For insurance claims and large commercial scopes we will tell you up front if a paid assessment is needed.

How fast can you get here for a water emergency?

Our emergency line is answered 24/7 and our typical on-site response is within two hours. Call rather than filling in the form if water is actively coming in — the phone is faster.

Do you work with insurance companies?

Constantly. We document moisture readings, drying logs and photos from day one, write scopes in the format adjusters expect, and will meet your adjuster on site. We work for you, not the carrier.

My house was built before 1978. Does that change things?

It can. Federal RRP rules require lead-safe work practices any time a pre-1978 surface is disturbed. We are an EPA Lead-Safe Certified Firm, so testing, containment and clearance are handled in-house.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — licensed in Maryland, DC and Virginia, bonded, and carrying general liability and workers' compensation. We send current certificates with every proposal, before you sign anything.

How long does a typical project take?

A deck runs two to four weeks, a bathroom four to six, a kitchen six to ten, and a whole-home renovation three to six months. Emergency mitigation starts the day you call. Your proposal includes a specific schedule, not a range.

Do you offer a warranty?

Two years on workmanship, plus whatever the manufacturer warranty is on materials. It is written into the contract, and we honor it without an argument.
